# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, unwelcome attention, publishing
someone's private information, or persistently derailing discussion after being
asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/yrameshk26/Tally/security/advisories/new)
or by opening an issue if it can be discussed publicly. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions, and may block
an account, at their discretion.

This is a small project maintained by one person in their spare time — enforcement
is best-effort and human, not a process.
